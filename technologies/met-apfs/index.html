MET-APFS: The Carrier And Its Feedback System

Instructional Biology

MET-APFS (Markosian Exosomal Transcytosis - Autonomic Pharmacokinetic Feedback System) is two technologies under one name. MET is an engineered exosome carrier that takes a therapeutic payload where it needs to go. APFS is a feedback system that follows what that payload does in the patient and adjusts the next dose. Each can be licensed on its own, or the two together.

A therapeutic is only as useful as its delivery. A signaling protein that degrades in circulation, is cleared before it reaches its tissue, or draws an immune response never gets to give its instruction. And a fixed dose, the same for everyone, ignores how differently each body handles it. MET-APFS answers both problems: it delivers the instruction intact, then measures and tunes how much of it each person receives.

MET: A Carrier For Any Payload

The MET carrier is modeled on the way the body's own cells ship molecules to one another in extracellular vesicles. Each carrier is built to a configuration record computed before manufacturing begins, which sets the targeting ligand it displays and the payload it carries.

The targeting on the outside is engineered separately from the cargo inside. That separation is what makes the carrier payload-agnostic: the same design is built to hold RNA, proteins or small molecules. A company with its own therapeutic can load it into the MET carrier without adopting anything else from our platform.

APFS: A Dose That Follows The Patient

APFS compares what the patient's digital twin predicted with what wearable sensors actually measure. When the two diverge, it proposes a revised dose, a revised payload composition or a revised dosing interval.

No proposal acts on its own authority. Each is checked against an authorization envelope the prescriber signs in advance, with a hard minimum, a hard maximum and a safe basal rate. A revision inside the envelope is signed, written to a write-once ledger and sent to the delivery device. A revision outside it is rejected and escalated to the prescriber, and the device falls back to the safe basal rate. APFS can reduce exposure on its own; any increase needs the prescriber's authorization.

APFS works with therapies other than ours. A company can license it to regulate the dosing of an unrelated treatment.

Licensable Apart Or Together

MET-APFS does not depend on our bio-processing claims, so it can be licensed without the rest of the platform:

  • The MET carrier loader, for a partner that wants to deliver its own payload.
  • APFS, for a partner that wants dosing to follow each patient in real time.
  • Both together, as the delivery and feedback layer of a personalized therapy.

Inside our own pipeline, MET-APFS is the fourth of the five connected systems. ASAP identifies the instruction, MHFBP extracts it without heat, ISRS-ML makes it safe without heat, MET-APFS delivers it, and the patient digital twin closes the loop.

The Patent Protection

The MET-APFS carrier loader is claimed in U.S. Patent Application No. 19/793,487 (claim 13), and the patient digital twin that APFS reports to is claimed in the same application (claim 26). The Exosome MET-APFS segment of our IP portfolio holds 47 planned applications. The claims are pending and have not been examined. See the IP Portfolio for how the five systems are protected.
