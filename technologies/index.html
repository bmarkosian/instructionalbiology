Instructional Biology Inc. — Technologies

A connected capability stack, not a single product.

No single algorithm or protocol translates a biological idea into a validated intervention on its own. Our platform is designed as a chain in which every layer can be examined, challenged, and improved independently.

The families do not stand or fall together.

Two of the five operate on third-party material and do not depend on the bioprocessing claims. A company with its own biologic could license either without touching the rest.

  • ASAP Discovery Part of the integrated chain
  • Bioprocessing Manufacture Part of the integrated chain
  • ISRS-ML Pathogen reduction Licensable on its own
  • MET-APFS Carrier Licensable on its own
  • Digital twin Dosing Part of the integrated chain

Nothing here is a granted patent. U.S. Patent Application No. 19/793,487 was filed 1 September 2026 and no claim has been examined or allowed.

01
Markosian High-Fidelity Bio-Processing Protocol (MHFBP)
Sentinel-protein thermal control designed to preserve native structure through isolation — the physical foundation the other four systems depend on having intact evidence to work with. This is the layer MHFBP governs.
02
Pathogen-reduction system
Ultrashort-pulse optical clearance, tuned below the threshold that damages protein structure — a non-thermal path to safety that doesn't trade away structural fidelity to get there.
03
Carrier (Exosome) manufacturing system
Engineered delivery vesicles, configured per individual by the same reasoning architecture that maps the underlying signaling biology.
04
Reasoning system
A hyperbolic knowledge graph — the Signaling Cascade Knowledge Graph, reasoned over by our Advanced Science AI Platform (ASAP) — linking signaling biology to individual outcome.
05
Therapeutic-regulation system
Closed-loop revision within a clinician-authorized envelope — bounded, reviewed, and never a fully autonomous decision a device makes on its own.

Why five systems, together

Each of these five systems solves a problem the others can't solve alone. A reasoning system without physically intact evidence to reason over will eventually run out of trustworthy input, because degraded material produces degraded data no algorithm can fully recover — which is why the bio-processing and pathogen-reduction systems come first in the chain, not as an afterthought bolted onto the science. A reasoning system with good evidence but no way to act on it is just an elaborate map; the carrier (exosome) manufacturing and therapeutic-regulation systems are what let a mapped hypothesis become something a clinician can actually use, inside boundaries they control.

We built the stack in this order deliberately, starting with the reasoning architecture: a system that can hold evidence at the right level of structure is valuable even before every downstream system is fully built out, because it changes how efficiently a research team can use whatever evidence it already has access to.
