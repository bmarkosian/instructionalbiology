The IP Architecture

How one parent application and its planned continuations protect each of the five systems of Instructional Medicine.

About This Film

How one parent application and its planned continuations protect each of the five systems, and why the native shape of the protein is the reason for all of it. Platform overview: forward-looking statements, not a claim of clinical results. 6 applications filed; 451 drafted and ready to file.

Where This Film Appears

  • IP Portfolio

Transcript

0:01 One parent patent application, five provisionals, 451 planned applications, 9,050 proposed claims. The architects of this intellectual property estate, Instructional Biology, operate on a specific premise. Traditional synthetic pharmacology and the practice of chemical reductionism are obsolete. They propose an alternative called Instructional Medicine.

0:27 This approach relies on natively folded biological blueprints to communicate instructions directly to the human body in its own evolutionary language. The entire scientific and legal strategy hinges on one strict biological constraint. A biological instruction only functions if its tertiary protein structure remains completely unbroken. Traditional biomanufacturing relies on heat, radiation, and mechanical shear forces.

0:56 Those standard procedures instantly denature and destroy these specific peptide configurations. This diagram illustrates the proposed solution, the Sovereign Biological Operating System. It is a five-step, closed-loop pipeline engineered entirely around protecting the fragility of these biological instructions, from initial in silico discovery all the way to patient specific dosing. Instructional Biology has mapped every individual node of this engineering pipeline into a ten-segment patent fortress.

1:29 The resulting master IP portfolio operates as a legally defensible blueprint for a vertically integrated commercial monopoly over this class of biologics. The logistical bottleneck of Instructional Medicine starts at the beginning, identifying the correct native instructions out of millions of potential biological signals. Claim 18 of the parent application secures the first node in the system. The platform avoids standard deep learning in favor of a non-Euclidean artificial intelligence architecture to pinpoint specific regulatory targets.

2:02 This computational engine uses hyperbolic embedding and signed anisotropic message passing. By navigating a directed signaling cascade knowledge graph, the AI isolates precise candidate interventions. Looking at this bar chart of planned patent applications grouped by commercial segment, we see exactly how this computational discovery engine is legally protected. Segment 7, labeled ASAP AI, holds a massive 101 separate applications dedicated to the discovery algorithm alone.

2:33 Once the AI identifies the target peptides in silico, the system must physically extract them from mammalian tissue without destroying their three-dimensional structure. This introduces Claim 1, the cyber-physical bioprocessing system. The target biologicals constitute a Thermosensitive Peptidome. Exposure to ambient processing heat permanently breaks their functional folds.

2:56 To prevent thermal denaturation, the hardware is governed by the Markosian Thermal Guardrail. Securing this hardware under Segment 6's 70 applications legally blocks competitors from building the required manufacturing machinery. Without physical thermal control, the AI's discoveries hold zero commercial value. Controlling the hardware ensures control over the entire market.

3:19 Once extracted, the biologic hits a regulatory wall. The FDA requires strict pathogen removal for patient safety. Yet standard sterilization protocols rely on heat or radiation, both of which cook the very peptide bonds the bioreactor just protected. The solution is documented in Claim 7, which introduces an optical pathogen reduction apparatus.

3:42 Solving the sterilization paradox is the critical bottleneck that makes a native state biologic viable. The density of the IP ring-fencing in Segment 3 reflects the commercial necessity of protecting that specific step. Even a perfectly sterilized native state peptide faces immediate destruction by the human immune system upon injection. The biologic requires a secure transit mechanism.

4:04 Claim 13 dictates the fourth node, an engineered exosome carrier system configured for payload-agnostic delivery. This engineered transcytosis carrier and its manufacturing methods are heavily defended by 47 applications in IP Segment 4. The pipeline concludes with Claim 26, which closes the biological loop at the point of patient administration. Wearable biosensors measure physiological signals in real time, comparing them against a patient's digital twin to automatically adjust the delivery device within a prescriber -set authorization envelope.

4:38 The logistical reality of scaling an FDA-regulated, closed-loop biological platform requires massive capital. The patent portfolio accounts for this by structuring immediate avenues for revenue. This chart divides clinical therapeutics from consumer goods. Segment 1 holds 14 OTC applications for cosmetics and supplements, generating early cash flow to bypass trial delays. That revenue fuels Segment 2's 36 applications targeting heavy disease indications, requiring extensive FDA approval, like treating opioid use disorder.

5:10 The system is designed to be commercially closed-loop. The intellectual property secures a path to harvest immediate OTC revenue, while simultaneously pursuing massive, highly regulated pharmaceutical endpoints. Zooming out from the biological mechanisms reveals the aggressive legal structure used to deploy these 9,050 claims. This timeline tracks priority dates.

5:34 Everything anchors to a parent application filed September 2026. From that parent, 160 continuations lock down the priority date, while 291 continuations in part dynamically expand the patent boundary as the technology scales. This is a swarm strategy. It traps potential competitors in a thicket of thousands of pending claims, well before the parent application is ever granted.

5:59 This entire legal architecture, the thousands of claims, and the five interlocking hardware systems exist solely because the native state peptide is fragile. This final diagram brings the five nodes together into one sovereign biological operating system. It demonstrates a strict lesson in modern intellectual property strategy. A single composition-of-matter patent is no longer sufficient.

6:24 To secure the market, a company must patent the discovery AI, the thermal bioreactor, the acoustic laser sterilization, the exosome carrier, and the digital twin dosing algorithm. By capturing every node of the Sovereign Biological Operating System, from computational discovery through physical manufacturing, Instructional Biology is engineering an inescapable vertical monopoly over this entire class of medicine.
