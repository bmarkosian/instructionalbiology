Architecting The Sovereign Biological Operating System

How Instructional Biology turns the body's own instructions into medicine, from in-silico discovery to the patient, and the patents built around every step.

About This Film

How Instructional Biology turns the body's own instructions into medicine, from in-silico discovery to the patient, and the patent architecture built around every step. Platform overview: forward-looking statements, not a claim of clinical results. 6 applications filed; 451 drafted and ready to file.

Transcript

0:01 The founder of Instructional Biology, Boris Markosian, states that chemical reductionism is obsolete, and the future of medicine is strictly instructional. Standard synthetic pharmacology operates through toxic chemical coercion, forcing physiological changes using foreign compounds. The alternative relies on the human body's preferred mechanism—native biological blueprints. These blueprints are homologous mammalian peptides.

0:30 Because they share identical structures with our own proteins, they communicate directly with the human body in its native evolutionary language. But capturing these instructions introduces a severe engineering bottleneck. Thermosensitive peptides are exceptionally fragile. When exposed to standard heat-based or chemical extraction methods, their delicate, folded structures rapidly unravel and violently break apart.

0:54 Harnessing these native biological instructions, therefore, requires the invention of entirely new, non-destructive bioprocessing physics. The solution to this manufacturing bottleneck is codified in the highly confidential instructional biology master IP portfolio. This diagram illustrates a vertically integrated, computationally driven closed loop governing the medicine lifecycle—digital discovery, physical extraction, optical sterilization, and real-time dosing. This ecosystem originates from one parent patent, with five distinct inventions protecting each node.

1:31 This architecture fractalizes into an impenetrable 9,000-claim fortress, covering 451 planned applications. By locking down every step of the process, this intellectual property structure guarantees a sovereign monopoly over both the digital algorithms that find the peptides and the physical manufacturing hardware that extracts them. This graphic shows the sequential map of the Instructional Medicine engine. We begin with node 1 in silico discovery, because extracting a native instruction first requires an exact digital blueprint.

2:06 Protected by patent claim 18, this node utilizes the Advanced Science AI Platform, or ASAP, to identify candidate therapeutic interventions computationally. To map complex native signaling cascades, the system embeds a directed signaling knowledge graph into a non-Euclidean manifold. The AI then executes a master regulator search by applying hyperbolic embedding and signed anisotropic message passing to navigate that manifold. This computational layer ensures that only highly precise, targeted biological instructions are identified and selected long before physical manufacturing ever begins.

2:45 Once the target is found, the challenge moves to physical extraction. Node 2, cyber-physical preservation, is governed by patent claim 1. The system then deploys thermodynamic cold-stop technology. This mechanism safely arrests the biological state during extraction, locking complex molecular shapes into place without fracturing them.

3:08 This cyber-physical integration solves the core biological bottleneck, clearing the path to industrialize native state protein manufacture. We then face a severe bioprocessing challenge, sterilizing a biological fluid without using any heat or chemicals. That happens here in Node 3, optical sterilization. This application of optical physics makes it possible to produce sterile, non-denatured biological medicine at an industrial scale.

3:37 The sterile payload must then survive the human bloodstream during delivery. This takes us to Node 4, engineered packaging, protected by patent claim 13. The system loads the peptides into a payload-agnostic engineered exosome carrier. The final step of the engine brings the medicine directly to the patient.

3:56 Node 5, digital twin regulation, is governed by patent claim 26. In this phase, wearable biosensors measure real-time physiological signals directly from the patient. Those physiological inputs are continuously compared against the patient's computational digital twin. The discrepancy between the real-time data and the digital model drives an adjustable dose delivery device.

4:20 This automated dosing remains strictly bound by an authorization envelope, set in advance by the prescriber. This final node closes the operational loop. It ensures that the biological instruction is delivered and constantly adjusted based on exact real-time physiological feedback. This unbroken chain of biological science maps directly onto an equally unbroken legal architecture.

4:44 This timeline illustrates how the strategy centralizes. All five distinct inventions are housed within a single filed parent application, U.S. 19/793,487, filed on September 1, 2026. Centralizing the inventions ensures every subsequent application in the portfolio inherits the earliest possible priority dates from original provisional filings.

5:08 This chart shows applications cascading from the parent node. 160 continuations and 291 continuations-in-part across 10 segments. This allows identical foundational hardware to secure monopolies in separate markets. For instance, segment 1 protects over-the-counter supplements, while segment 2 protects opioid use disorder treatments.

5:29 The commercial applications remain legally isolated. The master IP portfolio operates as a full-stack, 9,000-claim monopoly on the clinical translation of native mammalian signaling.
