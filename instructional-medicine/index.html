Instructional Biology — Instructional Medicine

Instructional Medicine.

The therapeutic framework behind everything Instructional Biology builds: the body's own signaling proteins, kept in their native shape from discovery to delivery, instructing cells toward the healing pathways programmed in our DNA.

What Instructional Medicine Is

Instructional Biology introduces a novel therapeutic framework categorized as Instructional Medicine. Rather than utilizing synthetic molecules to suppress biological pathways, the platform leverages the Thermosensitive Peptidome (TSP), biologics native to the body, to instruct cells towards healing pathways programmed in our DNA. These homologous instructional "blueprints" serve as exact biochemical instructions that are naturally utilized to orchestrate neuro-endocrine, immunological, and biological regenerative repair pathways which are capable of treating complex pathologies.

— Boris Markosian, Founder and CEO. Read the full Founder's Statement.

From Chemical Reductionism To Instruction

For over a century, modern medicine has relied on chemical reductionism: a synthetic compound engineered to block a receptor, shut down an enzyme or override a signaling cascade. Biological networks are not simple switches. Force one receptor and the network pushes back: receptors down-regulate, tolerance builds and side effects cascade through the body.

Instructional Medicine works the other way. It delivers the body's own signaling proteins, the Thermosensitive Peptidome, in their native shape, so they carry the same instructions the body already uses to coordinate repair. Shape is the medicine, which is why every step from discovery to delivery is built to keep that shape intact.

"Medicine is changing, we have evolved beyond chemical reductionism. The future of medicine is instructional."
— Boris Markosian, Founder

One Sovereign Biological Operating System

Instructional Medicine runs as one closed loop of five systems, each built to protect the native shape of the proteins it handles. Each system can also be licensed on its own.

  • ASAP: discovery. The Advanced Science AI Platform maps signaling biology on a Poincaré ball to find the native instructions worth testing.
  • MHFBP: preservation. The Markosian High-Fidelity Bio-Processing Protocol, with the Thermodynamic Cold-Stop, isolates signaling proteins without heat damage.
  • ISRS-ML: optical pathogen reduction. Makes biological material safe without heat, so the proteins come through the process intact.
  • MET-APFS (Markosian Exosomal Transcytosis - Autonomic Pharmacokinetic Feedback System): delivery. Two technologies, licensable separately or together, one of which connects to the patient's digital twin.
  • The digital twin: regulation. Wearable readings and a model of the individual keep dosing inside the limits a prescriber sets.

Protected As A Field

Instructional Medicine is set out in U.S. Patent Application No. 19/793,487, filed September 1, 2026, and in the five U.S. provisional applications filed before it, all naming Boris Markosian as sole inventor. Four of the five provisionals carry Instructional Medicine in their titles. The portfolio is built to protect the framework itself, not a single molecule.
