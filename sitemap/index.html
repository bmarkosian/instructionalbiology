Revenue Streams

  • Ten Revenue Streams

    Instructional Biology's ten revenue streams, the patent segments that protect them, and the USD 479.3 billion of markets those segments address.

  • ASAP

    ASAP, the Advanced Science AI Platform, is Instructional Biology's discovery engine, offered to pharma and biotech partners as subscriptions, discovery partnerships or platform licenses.

  • ISRS-ML

    ISRS-ML is Instructional Biology's non-thermal optical pathogen-reduction system, licensable as a standalone apparatus to blood banks, plasma processors and biologics manufacturers.

  • Thermodynamic Cold-Stop

    The Thermodynamic Cold-Stop: skids, manifolds and methods that arrest a process stream rapidly and without heat, supplied by Instructional Biology as a retrofit.

  • Bioreactor Retrofits

    Instructional Biology's Bioreactor Retrofits: jacketed vessels, retrofit skids, in-line structural sensors and guardrail control loops for existing bioreactors, licensed to contract manufacturers and equipment vendors.

  • OTC Products

    Instructional Biology's OTC Products: non-prescription dietary-supplement and transmucosal products, already at the Sales stage.

  • Cosmeceuticals

    Instructional Biology's Cosmeceuticals: non-prescription cosmetic products for the skin, scalp and hair, presented without any claim that they diagnose, treat, cure or prevent disease.

  • In-Silico Tox & Clinical

    Instructional Biology's In-Silico Tox & Clinical: computational toxicity screening and virtual-cohort testing for drug developers, combining ASAP with the digital twin before a study begins.

  • B2B Clinical

    Instructional Biology's B2B Clinical: digital twins for hospitals, clinics and prescribers, working only inside limits the prescriber authorizes.

  • B2C Wearables

    Instructional Biology's B2C Wearables: consumer wearable signal processing that turns a wearable's readings into a personal, informational report, with no recommendation to start, stop or change any medicine.

  • Therapeutics & Vanguard Assets

    Instructional Biology's Therapeutics & Vanguard Assets: drug candidates developed for disease indications, VPE-3 first, each protected by its indication, its composition, its carrier and its release certification.

  • Instructional Medicine: The Science
