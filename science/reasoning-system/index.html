Instructional Biology — The Science: System 04

The reasoning system.

A hyperbolic knowledge graph linking signaling biology to individual outcome — reasoned over by a graph neural network built to distinguish agonist from antagonist, and evidence from inference.

What this system actually does

The reasoning system is the part of our platform that takes a large, messy body of biological evidence — peptides, receptors, signaling cascades, master regulators, disease states, outcomes, and the directional, typed relationships between them — and organizes it into a structure a computational model can reason over explicitly, rather than holding it informally in one researcher's working memory.

We call the reasoning system ASAP, the Advanced Science AI Platform. ASAP is the operating system behind everything we build: the ISRS-ML module, the retrofit bioreactor skids, the MET-APFS (Markosian Exosomal Transcytosis - Autonomic Pharmacokinetic Feedback System) targeting system and the personalized-medicine digital twin. ASAP is to our platform what the processor is to a computer: it sits at the center and governs them all. Every safety stop runs in hardware on its own, so if ASAP ever goes silent, the process stops safely.

Two design choices make this possible. First, the underlying graph is embedded in a hyperbolic space rather than a flat, Euclidean one — a geometry that naturally accommodates the exponential branching of a real biological hierarchy without running out of representational room at the periphery, the way flat layouts do. Second, the reasoning layer on top of that embedding uses graph neural network message-passing that explicitly tracks relationship polarity — the difference between a peptide that activates a pathway and one that suppresses it — so that an activating relationship and an inhibiting one between the same two biological entities are never collapsed into a single, ambiguous signal.

The Frozen Graph, In Numbers

On November 4, 2026, the graph behind the reasoning system was frozen as its final record. Everything we build now runs on it.

  • 19,519 PubMed papers and 11,537 clinical trials behind it.
  • 12,835 nodes, from our 81 native signaling constituents through 973 receptors, 4,927 signaling-cascade nodes and 1,338 master regulators to 779 diseases and 4,736 disease phenotypes, joined by 35,004 edges.
  • A trained 128-dimensional Poincaré ball with mean distortion 0.0469 on held-out node pairs, within the 0.05 gate set before a map can be promoted.
  • 33,024 × 64 trained tangent angles: each signed edge turns its message by a trained angle in each of 64 planes of the receiving node's tangent space, so activation and inhibition keep their full strength instead of averaging away. Each node's tangent space can now be viewed in 2D and 3D.
  • 2,409 diseases and disease phenotypes with new predicted-benefit calls (19,251 calls), where the record has no direct link, no shared paper and no tested trial. It also flags contraindications: 14,269 across all pairs scored.
  • 20 demo patient digital twins, each a simulated subject.

Computational results, not clinical results. Every call carries its route, its path strength and its certainty, and is a hypothesis to test. Read how ASAP was trained.

What the reasoning system outputs, and what it doesn't

Given a candidate biological question, the reasoning system produces a ranked set of hypotheses — connections between a signaling molecule and a possible outcome, weighted by how well-supported the connecting evidence is and how central the intermediate biology is to the broader signaling network. It does not produce a diagnosis, a treatment decision, or a claim of efficacy. Every output is designed to be traceable back to the evidence that supports it, reviewed by a human before it goes anywhere near a laboratory bench, let alone a patient.

This is the layer every other system in our stack ultimately depends on. The carrier (exosome) manufacturing and therapeutic-regulation systems both take their individualized configuration from what this system maps; the bio-processing and pathogen-reduction systems exist to make sure the physical evidence feeding it hasn't already been degraded before it arrives.
