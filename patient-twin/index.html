THE AVERAGE FAILS TWICE

Most drugs never reach patients. The ones that do help few of the people who take them.

About 7 in 100 drugs that enter human trials are approved (6.7%, 2014–2023). And some of the best-selling drugs in America help between 1 in 25 and 1 in 4 of the people who take them. Medicine built on the average fails most people twice: in the trial, and in the clinic.

6.7%of drugs entering Phase 1 reach approval (Citeline, 2014–2023). Biologics have historically done about twice as well.

TWO TWINS WE WILL OFFER

One for everyday choices. One for prescribed care.

Both are built on the same trial evidence and the same body-stress signal. They differ in who acts on what the twin sees.

NON-PRESCRIPTION · WELLNESS

Instructional Twin

Runs on the wearables people already own. It shows what the twin predicted next to what the body actually did, so a person can choose what to do about sleep, activity and the everyday factors that move their stress signal.

It never administers anything.

PRESCRIBED · CLOSED LOOP

Therapeutic Twin

Works under a prescriber's care. The doctor sets the minimum and maximum dose, and the twin adjusts dosing only inside those limits as the body's signals change. Every decision is recorded.

It doses only inside the limits a prescriber has approved.
