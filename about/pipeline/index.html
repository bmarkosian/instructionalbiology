Pipeline · Updated October 29, 2026

Our Pipeline

Instructional Biology Inc.

Each of our ten revenue streams moves along the same seven stages: Discovery, Development, Testing, Prototype, Ready for Demo, IND (if required) and Sales. IND is an Investigational New Drug application, filed only where a stream requires one. Each stream's arrow runs from Discovery to the stage it has reached.

Where each revenue stream stands

  • ASAP: Ready for Demo. Our Advanced Science AI Platform, which reasons over the Signaling Cascade Knowledge Graph. Read about the reasoning system.
  • ISRS-ML: Discovery. Non-thermal optical pathogen reduction for blood banks, plasma processors and biologics manufacturers. Read about pathogen reduction.
  • Thermodynamic Cold-Stop: Development. Skids, manifolds and methods that arrest a process stream rapidly and without heat, supplied as a retrofit.
  • Bioreactor Retrofits: Development. Retrofit skids, in-line structural sensors and guardrail control loops for existing bioreactors. Read about bio-manufacturing.
  • OTC Products: Sales. Our over-the-counter products.
  • Cosmeceuticals: Development. Non-prescription cosmetic products for the skin, scalp and hair.
  • In-Silico Tox & Clinical: Testing. Computational toxicity screening and virtual-cohort testing for drug developers, combining ASAP with the digital twin. Read about in-silico testing.
  • B2B Clinical: Discovery. Digital twins for hospitals, clinics and prescribers, working only inside limits the prescriber authorizes. Read about digital twins.
  • B2C Wearables: Development. Wearable readings turned into a personal, informational report. Read about wearable health monitoring.
  • Therapeutics & Vanguard Assets: Discovery. Drug candidates for disease indications, VPE-3 first: our research program on strengthening the brain's response to opioids, studied so far in rats. Read about VPE-3.

For partnership or licensing conversations about any stream, see Partnerships and Licensing.

Stages reflect Instructional Biology's own development tracking as of October 29, 2026. VPE-3 has not been tested in people, and nothing on this page is medical advice.
