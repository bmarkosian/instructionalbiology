Pipeline · Updated October 28, 2026

Our Pipeline

Instructional Biology Inc.

Every Instructional Biology program moves along the same seven stages: Discovery, Development, Testing, Prototype, Ready for Demo, IND (if required) and Sales. IND is an Investigational New Drug application, filed only where a program requires one. Each program's arrow runs from Discovery to the stage it has reached.

Where each program stands

  • ASAP: Ready for Demo. Our Advanced Science AI Platform, which reasons over the Signaling Cascade Knowledge Graph. Read about the reasoning system.
  • VPE-3: Discovery. Our research program on strengthening the brain's response to opioids, studied so far in rats. Read about VPE-3.
  • OTC: Sales. Our over-the-counter products.
  • Digital Twins: Testing. A model of one person that learns with them. Read about digital twins.

For partnership or licensing conversations about any program, see Partnerships and Licensing.

Stages reflect Instructional Biology's own development tracking as of October 28, 2026. VPE-3 has not been tested in people, and nothing on this page is medical advice.
