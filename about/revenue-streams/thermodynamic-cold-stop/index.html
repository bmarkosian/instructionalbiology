Thermodynamic Cold-Stop

Instructional Biology Inc.

The Thermodynamic Cold-Stop is a set of skids, manifolds and methods that arrest a process stream rapidly and without heat. Instructional Biology supplies it as a retrofit, so it is added to process equipment a manufacturer already runs.

Where It Stands

The Thermodynamic Cold-Stop is at Development, the second of the seven stages on Our Pipeline: Discovery, Development, Testing, Prototype, Ready for Demo, IND (if required) and Sales.

Why Heat Is the Enemy

A protein's biological function depends on its three-dimensional fold. That fold can be disrupted by heat, by extreme pH or by mechanical shear long before any covalent bond in the molecule breaks: the amino-acid sequence survives, and the shape that made it work does not. Why Does Heat Denature Proteins? explains the chemistry in plain language.

Its Role in MHFBP

In the Markosian High-Fidelity Bio-Processing Protocol (MHFBP), the Thermodynamic Cold-Stop is the thermal guardrail. It engages throughout isolation so the molecules being processed never experience the heat that would unravel their folded shape. Every safety stop runs in hardware on its own, so if ASAP ever goes silent, the process stops safely. The Cold-Stop works alongside our Bioreactor Retrofits.

The Segment That Protects It

The Thermodynamic Cold-Stop is protected by its own segment of our IP portfolio. It is claimed together with MHFBP in U.S. Patent Application No. 19/793,487 (claim 1). The claims are pending and have not been examined.
