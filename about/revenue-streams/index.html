Revenue streams · Updated October 29, 2026

Ten Revenue Streams

Instructional Biology

Our IP portfolio is organized into ten segments, and the business into ten revenue streams. Each revenue stream is protected by one or more of those segments, so a stream can be licensed, supplied or provided as a service on its own, without encumbering the others. Some segments, such as Composition of Matter and Provenance and Release, protect several streams at once. Where each program stands today is shown on Our Pipeline, and each stream below has its own page.

The ten revenue streams

01. ASAP

The Advanced Science AI Platform, our discovery engine, offered to pharma and biotech partners as subscriptions, discovery partnerships or platform licenses. Protected by: ASAP AI. Read more about ASAP.

02. ISRS-ML

Non-thermal optical pathogen reduction, licensable as a standalone apparatus to blood banks, plasma processors and biologics manufacturers. Protected by: ISRS-ML. Read more about ISRS-ML.

03. Thermodynamic Cold-Stop

Skids, manifolds and methods that arrest a process stream rapidly and without heat, supplied as a retrofit. Protected by: Thermodynamic Cold-Stop. Read more about Thermodynamic Cold-Stop.

04. Bioreactor Retrofits

Jacketed vessels, retrofit skids, in-line structural sensors and guardrail control loops for existing bioreactors, licensed to contract manufacturers and equipment vendors. Protected by: Bioreactor Skids. Read more about Bioreactor Retrofits.

05. OTC Products

Non-prescription dietary-supplement and transmucosal products. Our over-the-counter products are already at the Sales stage. Protected by: OTC Products, Composition of Matter, Provenance and Release. Read more about OTC Products.

06. Cosmeceuticals

Non-prescription cosmetic products for the skin, scalp and hair, presented without any claim that they diagnose, treat, cure or prevent disease. Protected by: OTC Products, Composition of Matter, Provenance and Release. Read more about Cosmeceuticals.

07. In-Silico Tox & Clinical

Computational toxicity screening and virtual-cohort testing for drug developers, combining ASAP with the digital twin before a study begins. Protected by: ASAP AI, Digital Twin. Read more about In-Silico Tox & Clinical.

08. B2B Clinical

Digital twins for hospitals, clinics and prescribers, working only inside limits the prescriber authorizes. Protected by: Digital Twin. Read more about B2B Clinical.

09. B2C Wearables

Consumer wearable signal processing that turns a wearable's readings into a personal, informational report, with no recommendation to start, stop or change any medicine. Protected by: Digital Twin. Read more about B2C Wearables.

10. Therapeutics & Vanguard Assets

Drug candidates developed for disease indications, VPE-3 first, each protected by its indication, its composition, its carrier and its release certification. Protected by: Disease Indications, Exosome MET-APFS (Markosian Exosomal Transcytosis - Autonomic Pharmacokinetic Feedback System), Composition of Matter, Provenance and Release. Read more about Therapeutics & Vanguard Assets.

How the streams are metered

Every stream is designed to be metered on an immutable ledger rather than sold once as a delivered artifact: by record emitted, by volume treated, by lot released, by installation-hour or by subject enrolled. A license can exclude a defined field of use, and the control system will not run a recipe that falls inside an excluded field.

For partnership or licensing conversations about any stream, see Partnerships and Licensing.
