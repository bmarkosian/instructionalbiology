Instructional Biology Inc. — Partnerships & Licensing

Interested in partnering or licensing?

We're open to conversations with research, clinical, and pharmaceutical partners interested in any of our five core technology systems — individually or as a whole.

What kinds of conversations we're having

Instructional Biology's technology stack was built as five distinct systems on purpose — a research institution interested in our reasoning architecture and its knowledge-graph methodology has a very different conversation with us than a manufacturing partner interested in the bio-processing or pathogen-reduction systems, and both are different again from a pharmaceutical partner exploring co-development or licensing of the full stack. We'd rather have the right conversation than force every inquiry through one generic intake process.

If you're not sure which category your interest falls into, that's fine — start the conversation and we'll help figure out the right shape for it together.

The investor reference room

Our public site is deliberately built to explain the vision without exposing protected work or getting ahead of announcements we haven't made yet. For qualified reviewers — investors, prospective partners, and licensees who've had an introductory conversation with us — we maintain a controlled virtual data room with additional non-public material.

Investor reference room

Access to the virtual data room is granted after an introductory conversation, on a qualified, case-by-case basis. There's nothing behind this yet for unqualified visitors — that's intentional, not a bug.

Approach the vault

How to start

Email us directly, or use the contact page, and tell us plainly what you're interested in — which system, what kind of arrangement you're picturing, and a bit about your organization. We'll follow up to set up an introductory conversation, and go from there.

This page describes our general openness to partnership and licensing conversations. It is not an offer, solicitation, or commitment of any kind, and access to any non-public material is granted at our sole discretion following direct qualification.
